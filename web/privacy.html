Overview

Ngoding Lok ("the App", "we", "us") is an AI-assisted, gamified platform that teaches foundational programming concepts. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the App at ngoding-lok.web.app or through our mobile application, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Automatically collected technical data. Like most websites and apps, our infrastructure and service providers automatically receive standard technical information such as your IP address, device and browser type, operating system, approximate region, referring pages, and interaction timestamps.
  • Usage data. Non-identifying information about how you interact with lessons and features (for example, which challenges you open or when you request a hint) so we can improve the learning experience.
  • Advertising identifiers. To display ads, our advertising partners may access cookies and, on mobile, a resettable advertising ID. See Advertising & Cookies below.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not ask for sensitive personal details to use the App.

  • These technologies may be used to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your consent and settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses cookies in advertising.
